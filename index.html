Our Story

How We Met

Our story began in the Philippines, where we worked together as graphic designers. What started as a simple workplace friendship gradually grew into something truly special.

As colleagues, we spent our days working side by side, but it was Yna who made the first move by asking Paul to be her work buddy. That simple gesture sparked a connection that would eventually change both of our lives.

Over time, we grew closer through the little moments that became our favorite memories. We shared countless cups of coffee, watched movies together, explored the city through cycling adventures and spontaneous drives, traveled to other countries together, and discovered a mutual love for cooking and sharing meals.

What began as friendship developed naturally into a relationship built on trust, companionship, laughter, and love. Through every adventure, both big and small, we found a home in each other.
